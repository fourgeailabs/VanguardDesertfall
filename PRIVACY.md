# Privacy Policy — Vanguard: Desertfall

*Last updated: October 27, 2026 · Developer: FourgeAI LABS*

Vanguard: Desertfall is an offline single-player game. This policy is short because the game
was designed to have nothing to collect.

## What data we collect
**None.** Vanguard: Desertfall does not collect, transmit, store on any server, sell, or share
any personal data or usage data of any kind.

- The app requests **zero device permissions** — including no internet access. It is technically
  incapable of sending your data anywhere.
- The app contains **no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or social SDKs** and no
  third-party code that receives data.
- There are **no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, and no in-app purchases**.

## What the game stores on your device
The game saves only your gameplay progress (which missions you've completed) and your settings
(volumes, sensitivity, difficulty, subtitles) in local storage on your device. This information
never leaves your device. You can erase it at any time via
**Android Settings → Apps → Vanguard: Desertfall → Storage → Clear storage**, or by uninstalling
the game.

## Children
Because the game collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

## Changes to this policy
If a future version of the game ever adds a feature involving data (for example an online
leaderboard), this policy and the game's Play Store data-safety declaration will be updated
before that version is released.
