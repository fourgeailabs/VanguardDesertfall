## Full description
**VANGUARD: DESERTFALL** — a fully offline 3D first-person shooter campaign for Android.

Alt-history Arizona. A techno-fascist Empire won its war with dieselpunk super-weapons and
occupies the American Southwest, watching every street from its comm towers. You are **Cole**,
a former telecom lineman turned resistance fighter — the one person who can climb those towers,
splice into the Empire's own uplink, and turn its eyes against it. Your handler is **Mara Voss**.

Across **five missions** you'll fight on foot and behind the wheel through Surprise, the desert
highways, occupied Phoenix, a secret mesa laboratory, and the Empire's chrono-engine citadel:

- **Mission 1 — First Tower:** hack a comm tower and defend it
- **Mission 2 — Iron Convoy:** drive the armoured '94 Suburban, then blow a substation
- **Mission 3 — The Mesa Lab:** robot hounds, a chrono core, and a race against time itself
- **Mission 4 — Phoenix Down:** capture three comm relays in any order
- **Mission 5 — The Paradox Engine:** face the Siege Walker and end the occupation

**Features**
- 100% **offline** campaign — no account, no server, no ads, no in-app purchases
- **Fully voiced cutscenes** — real conversations between Mara and Cole drive the story
- **Positional 3D audio** — hear shots from where they're actually fired
- The **Vanguard Tether** — grapple to towers and rooftops, Spider-Man style
- **Full Bluetooth and wired gamepad support**, plus touch controls
- Positional weapons with distinct handling: rifle, scattergun, and the Empire Heavy Repeater
- Difficulty settings (Story / Normal / Veteran) and full audio/subtitle controls

**Honest beta note:** this is an internal-testing beta from a two-person team (a developer and
his AI). Art is stylised geometry with procedural textures, the sound effects and score are
synthesized, and character voices are generated. Balance is still being tuned with tester
feedback — thank you for playing early and telling us what feels wrong.

**MATURE 17+ — for adults.** Contains intense, frequent violence against humanoid enemy
combatants and machines in a fictional alt-history war setting, including **blood and gore
effects**. This is a war game and it does not soften that. No profanity in dialogue; no sexual
content; no gambling.
