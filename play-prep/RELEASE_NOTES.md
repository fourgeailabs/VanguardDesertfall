# Internal Testing Release Notes — Vanguard: Desertfall 0.4.0-beta

## What's new (paste into Play Console → Internal testing → Release notes)

Welcome to the first Desertfall beta! 🌀

• The complete 5-mission offline campaign: comm towers, a driving mission in an armoured
  '94 Suburban, robot hounds, a secret mesa lab, and a mech boss finale
• Fully voiced cutscene conversations (Mara & Cole) in every mission
• Positional audio, difficulty settings, camera-shake and subtitle options
• Full Bluetooth + wired gamepad support, plus touch controls

This is an early beta from a two-person team: art is stylised geometry, sounds are
synthesized, voices are generated, and balance hasn't been tuned on real phones yet.
Please play Missions 1–3 first and tell us how it FEELS — gunplay, driving, the hound,
controller deadzones, frame rate. Thank you!

## Known limits (honest, for testers)
- **Nothing has been playtested on a physical device before this beta** — frame rate, game feel,
  gamepad behaviour on specific controllers, and difficulty balance are all unverified. That's
  exactly what this test is for.
- Art is a box-geometry engine with procedural textures and blob shadows — no skeletal
  animation, no shadow mapping. It is stylised on purpose, and it's still evolving.
- All sound effects and music are **synthesized** placeholders (original, generated in code);
  Mara and Cole are **generated voices**, not human actors.
- Enemies use simple collision and line-of-sight, not pathfinding — a walled-off enemy may hug
  a wall until it can see you.
- Chrono Remix mode (story-seeded in Mission 3) is **not** in this build — post-launch roadmap.
- Progress is stored only on your device. Uninstalling deletes your campaign progress.
